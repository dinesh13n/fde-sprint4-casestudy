# Historical operations notes — Pharma

2018: migrated from spreadsheet approvals to the current service.

2020: added partner data ingestion; replays from external integrations remain operationally common.

2022: introduced a revised region mapping. Local reports retain historical region labels.

2024: operations requested stricter approvals after a dispute, but a parallel workflow was retained for older customers.

2025: a migration initiative was paused after compatibility concerns with an upstream system.

Several reconciliations remain manual. Support requests can refer to either case identifiers or domain-specific identifiers.
