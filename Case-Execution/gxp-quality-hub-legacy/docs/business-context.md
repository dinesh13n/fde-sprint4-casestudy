# Life Sciences — Pharma Quality Intelligence

Fictional customer: **Gxp Group**, a multi-region organization with inherited operational systems. All people and company records are synthetic.

## Operating concern

Batch quality review and deviation processing. Operations leadership reports rising exception-handling effort and inconsistent information between external feeds and the core system.

## Operating stakeholders

- Quality operations lead: throughput, handling time, case quality
- Enterprise application owner: uptime, interfaces, release process
- Finance partner: case cost and avoidable rework
- Risk and security partner: authorization, retention, audit evidence
- Integration lead: delivery semantics and reconciliation

## Discovery material

The database includes cases, operational events, external source messages and monthly cost estimates. Interview notes are incomplete; local interpretations of a 'closed' case differ between sites.

## Existing workflow

1. External sources produce case messages.
2. The integration process reconciles or imports them into the legacy system.
3. Operations users review and prioritize work.
4. Case decisions are recorded in local systems.
5. Monthly operating measures are reviewed.

## Transformation scope

The existing system is the current state. Determine and justify any modernization or AI intervention through business and technical evidence, preserving legitimate workflows.
